BISNU-X Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

BISNU-X is an AI-powered Android application designed to provide AI assistance, information discovery, search, scanning and productivity features.

1. Information We May Collect

BISNU-X may process information that you provide while using the application, including account information, authentication information, messages or queries submitted to the service, and information required to provide requested features.

2. AI and Search Features

Queries and related content may be processed by the BISNU-X backend and third-party services required to provide AI, search or related functionality.

3. Camera, Microphone and Files

If a feature requires camera, microphone or file access, the application requests the relevant Android permission. Such access is used only to provide the requested functionality.

4. Payments and Premium Features

BISNU-X may offer Premium features. Payment processing may be provided by Razorpay. Payment credentials and Razorpay secret keys are not stored inside the Android application. Premium entitlement is determined by server-side verification.

5. Advertising

BISNU-X may use Google AdMob or other advertising services to display advertisements. Advertising providers may process device or advertising-related information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through BISNU-X. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

BISNU-X may use third-party services for authentication, AI processing, search, payments, hosting, databases and advertising. These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

9. Children's Privacy

BISNU-X is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Your Choices

You may stop using BISNU-X at any time. Where applicable, you may request deletion or correction of personal information through the available support channel.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the BISNU-X support team through the official support/contact channel provided with the application.
